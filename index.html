How to read the numbers. “Not to exceed” figures are authorization ceilings — permission to spend up to an amount, not payments. Other figures are amounts printed in the resolution text, quoted exactly. Checkbook rows are actual warrant payments, void and credit lines excluded. Every lead links to the government’s own source document. Erie notes: most floor adoptions are voice votes — the minutes print an outcome but no tally, and the cards show exactly that; committee votes are shown where the record prints them; defeated floor items never receive resolution numbers in Erie’s minutes, so they cannot appear as voted leads. Town of Tonawanda notes: the minutes print a status word plus mover and seconder but no per-member tally (verified in the text layer), and the cards show exactly that; bid awards print the winner and the full tabulation — including losing bidders’ prices — so “awarded to” there is the record’s own language; money adopted subject to a permissive referendum shows its 30-day unlock clock; one malformed dollar token in Res 2026-471 (“$10,0000,000.00”) is excluded from every total rather than guessed into a number.
