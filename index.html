How to read the numbers. “Not to exceed” figures are authorization ceilings — permission to spend up to an amount, not payments. Other figures are amounts printed in the resolution text, quoted exactly. Checkbook rows are actual warrant payments, void and credit lines excluded. Every lead links to the government’s own source document. Erie notes: most floor adoptions are voice votes — the minutes print an outcome but no tally, and the cards show exactly that; committee votes are shown where the record prints them; defeated floor items never receive resolution numbers in Erie’s minutes, so they cannot appear as voted leads. Parsed by the same engine behind the Public Ledger civic project — where the record is unclear, we say so rather than guess.
